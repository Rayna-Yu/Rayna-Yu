Welcome to my GitHub! I'm a Computer Science and Math student at Northeastern University with a minor in Robotics who loves building backend systems that are fast, reliable, and scalable. Most recently, I interned at AWS on the EBS team, and I currently lead a Code 4 Community team migrating 141 years of weather data for the Blue Hill Observatory. I'm excited to keep growing in backend areas like distributed systems and cloud infrastructure, along with algorithms, while contributing to projects that make a real-world impact.
